Chart Monitor gives you a clear view of stocks, ETFs and indices – with charts, technical indicators, analyst ratings and a built-in screener. Free, no ads, no sign-up.

CHARTS
• Line and candlestick charts, switchable per stock
• Compare two stocks directly – percentage-normalized in one chart
• Time ranges from 1 day to max, or your own custom range
• Tap or drag to see the exact price and date

TECHNICAL INDICATORS
• Moving averages MA20, MA50 and MA200
• Bollinger Bands
• Trend line over the selected period
• Analyst price target as a line in the chart
• Your settings are remembered

STOCK ANALYSIS
• Overall star rating for performance, risk, fundamentals, technicals and trading suitability
• Analyst recommendations from "Strong Buy" to "Strong Sell"

COMPANY DETAILS
• Sector, industry, country, CEO, employees, market cap
• Valuation: P/E, EPS, beta, 52-week high and low, next earnings date
• Dividends: dividend per share, yield, ex-date and dividend history
• Latest news and company description
• For ETFs: top holdings and sector weighting

STOCK SCREENER
• Filter stocks by performance and P/E ratio
• Universes such as S&P 500, Nasdaq 100, Russell 2000, NYSE, DAX 40, SMI 20 and FTSE 100

WATCHLIST
• Save your favorites with one tap
• Performance per period, sector badges and several sort options
• Stop-loss and target price per stock – you are notified when you open the watchlist and a level has been reached
• Personal notes, optionally linked to a stock

INDICES
• Major world indices at a glance
• Index comparison as a bar chart, optionally including your watchlist

FX CALCULATOR
• Currency converter with live rates
• Interest and compound interest calculator with deposits and withdrawals

GENERAL
• Light and dark mode (follows system setting)
• English and German
• No personal data, no account, no ads

Chart Monitor

Free stock chart app for Android

⬇ Download APK

Open APK → Install. Also works as an update over an existing installation.

Features
📊 Line & candlestick charts
🔀 Two-stock comparison (%-normalized)
📅 1D to Max + custom date range
📉 MA20/38/50/200, Trend, Target, Bollinger Bands, RSI
⭐ Stock analysis with 5-star rating
🔔 Watchlist with price alerts
🏢 Company info, dividends, news
💱 FX calculator (currency & interest)
🌐 German & English
